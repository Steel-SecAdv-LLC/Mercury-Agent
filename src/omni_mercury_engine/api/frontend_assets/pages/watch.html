A finding is reported when a published scale says so (USGS magnitude classes, the NOAA G scale, a tsunami buoy's own event mode, the NWS flood category, the USGS volcano alert levels). Most reads report nothing.

Who is told

The watch sends each finding to the people it concerns: what happened within their radius of the place they gave, and every National Weather Service alert in effect there. To be told, sign in and name a place under Watch alerts on your account page; whoever runs this server can also subscribe people with mercury-agent alerts add. Mercury does not issue warnings; in an emergency call your emergency number.

Track record

Mercury checks each finding later against the record that settles it: the USGS catalogue once a seismologist has reviewed an earthquake, NOAA's tsunami database, the same gauge's later readings.
